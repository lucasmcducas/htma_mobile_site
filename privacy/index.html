Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data HTMA Mobile collects, why, who sees it, and what you can do about it. It covers the Android app and this website.

Last updated 22 October 2026 · Applies to HTMA Mobile v0.1.1 and later · Operated by PRYOR ASSETS, LLC

Plain version: your mineral chart and the notes you write stay in our database. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers. When you ask our AI assistant a question, the question and the relevant parts of your chart are sent to the AI provider that answers you — that is the only reason they are processed. We ask you for permission the first time, and you can switch the assistant off at any time. You can export or delete your data at any time.

1. What we collect

Only what the app needs to do its job.

DataWhenWhy
Account — your email address and a password When you create an account So you can sign in and so we can reach you about your account. Your password is hashed; we never store it in readable form.

What we do not collect

No advertising identifiers, no location, no contacts, no microphone recording saved to our servers, no browsing history, no third-party advertising or data-broker profiles.

2. Who we share it with

We share the minimum needed to operate the service:

  • Supabase — our database, authentication and the function that runs the AI assistant. It processes data on our instructions under a written data-processing agreement. Your data stays in the United States.
  • Stripe — payments and subscriptions. Stripe handles card data; we receive only a confirmation and the last four digits.
  • Sentry — crash reporting. Personal information is disabled.
  • MiniMax — the AI provider that answers your questions. It receives only what section 4 describes. MiniMax is operated by Nanonoble Pte. Ltd. (Singapore), which states that API data is stored in a data centre in the United States. See section 4 for exactly what it receives and what happens to it.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it for anyone else's advertising. We do not share identifiable health data with data brokers, advertisers, or insurers.

We may disclose data if we are legally required to. We will tell you unless we are prohibited from doing so.

3. Where your data lives

Stored in the United States, on infrastructure operated by our database provider, with access limited to the people who operate the service.

Encryption in transit uses HTTPS. Access is controlled by account. Every account is a separate record; one user cannot read another's data.

4. How our AI assistant works

When you ask the assistant a question about your chart, the question and the relevant parts of your chart are sent to our own server, which passes them to MiniMax to produce an answer.

What is sent

Your name, your age, your sex, and your mineral values and the patterns computed from them — enough for the assistant to interpret your chart. Your notes, your symptom log, your doses and your uploaded report photo or PDF are not sent to the AI provider. If you dictate a question, only the text is sent, not audio.

Your permission

The first time you use the assistant, the app shows you exactly what will be sent and asks for your permission before anything leaves your phone. You can then switch the assistant off in Settings at any time, and switching it off stops the sharing immediately. You are never asked again unless the list of what is shared changes.

What happens to it at MiniMax

We want to be precise here rather than reassuring, because this is health data.

MiniMax's terms for API customers say that you keep ownership of the input and the generated content, and that MiniMax may use that input and content to provide, maintain, develop and improve its services, to comply with law, to enforce its terms, and to keep its services safe. MiniMax's privacy policy separately states that it does not use your input personal data to infer characteristics about an individual, and does not use personal data for training to profile or target consumers. It states that API data is stored in a data centre in the United States, and that data may be transferred to other countries. It does not publish a fixed retention period for API data; its stated basis is how long the data is needed or permitted to be kept for the purposes it was collected.

We are not able to give you a stronger promise than that. What we can tell you is that we do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and you can turn the assistant off.

The assistant is instructed to describe your chart, not to diagnose you or prescribe treatment.

Voice input

If you dictate a question, your phone's built-in speech recognition converts it to text. Depending on your phone and language settings, that conversion may happen on your device or be sent to the speech service your phone maker provides. Dictating is always optional — you can type every question instead.

Crash reports

Crash reporting is configured not to send personal information — no email, no name, no report content. Reports include the app version, device model and the error itself.

5. Your choices and rights

Export your data

You can export your chart and your log from inside the app at any time. You can send that export to your practitioner, or keep it.

Delete your data

You can request deletion of your account and everything in it. Write to us and we will delete it. See section 7.

Withdraw a report

Removing a report from your account does not by itself settle what happens to copies you have already shared. If you have sent your report or your data to your practitioner, they hold their own copy under their own privacy practices. Tell us and we will tell you what we can do.

Do not track

We do not track you across other websites and apps. There is nothing to opt out of, because we do not do it.

6. Children

HTMA Mobile is a general consumer app. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from a child who is using it without a parent or guardian.

A parent or guardian may create an account to hold their child's report — the app supports a subject other than the account holder, which is what makes that possible. When you do that, you are the account holder and you are responsible for the information in it. If you believe a child has given us personal information without a parent or guardian's involvement, write to us and we will delete it.
